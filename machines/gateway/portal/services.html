MAKE YOURSELF COMFORTABLE

A little help.

Signing in to a service

Choose Sign in with Pocket ID if a service asks you to sign in. Your existing Pocket ID session may let you continue without another passkey prompt.

Watching on a TV

Enter https://jellyfin.flaked.org in your Jellyfin-compatible TV app. Choose Quick Connect, if available.

Open Jellyfin on your phone, sign in, then enter the TV’s code in your profile’s Quick Connect setting. Only approve a code from your own device.

Managing your account

Pocket ID is where you manage your passkeys. Contact the person who invited you if you can no longer sign in.

Using a shared device?

Sign out of home closes this page’s session only. Also sign out of any services you opened and your Pocket ID account.
