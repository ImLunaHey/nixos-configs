A HAND GETTING STARTED

Less setup.
More switching off.

Signing in for the first time

Open your private invitation and follow the steps to save a passkey. Then open Jellyfin and choose Sign in with Pocket ID.

Use your device’s face recognition, fingerprint or screen lock when prompted. Keep your invitation link private.

Watching on a TV

In a Jellyfin-compatible TV app, enter https://jellyfin.flaked.org as the server and choose Quick Connect, if available.

Sign in to Jellyfin on your phone. Open your profile’s Quick Connect setting and enter the code displayed on your TV. Only approve a code from your own device.

Managing your passkeys

Open your Pocket ID account to manage your passkeys. If you can no longer sign in, contact the person who invited you.

Stuck on “Logging in…”?

Return to Jellyfin and start a fresh sign-in. Refreshing an old sign-in callback can reuse an expired link.
